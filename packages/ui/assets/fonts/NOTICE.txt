Inter (weights 400, 500, 600, 700) and Poppins (weights 600, 700) are
subsets of the SIL Open Font License fonts. Full license: OFL.txt.
Poppins 500 is listed on the design's Google Fonts URL and is not used
by any CSS rule, so it is not bundled.

The subset covers Latin-1, general punctuation, the rupee sign, and the
middle dot, ellipsis, dashes, and bullet used in the design. Inter and
Poppins do not contain Indic glyphs. Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu,
Marathi, and Kannada strings in config.json fall back to the platform font.

Lucide.ttf is the regular Lucide icon font (one stroke weight, matching
the design's 2px stroke). Wrapper license: LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt.
